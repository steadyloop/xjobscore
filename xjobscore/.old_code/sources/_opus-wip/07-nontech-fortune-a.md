## 7. Tech Roles at Non-Tech Companies — Part A: Retail, CPG, Healthcare, Pharma, Hospitality, Food, Agriculture

This is the highest-leverage, lowest-competition family in the entire catalog. A grocery chain, a health insurer, and a seed company each run SWE/data/ML intern classes in the dozens-to-hundreds, and they are almost never on the "top 50 tech internships" lists that saturate applicant pools. The catch is fragmentation: there is no single board, so ingestion means enumerating ~200 employer career portals and reducing them to ~8 ATS families (Workday, Phenom, Radancy TalentBrew, Eightfold, Greenhouse, iCIMS, Avature, SuccessFactors/Taleo). Once you write one adapter per ATS, each new company costs you one row of config — that is the entire economic argument for this section. Effort profile: high one-time build, near-zero marginal cost per employer, and yields postings 4–10 weeks before they surface on aggregators.

Confidence markers appear inline in Notes: `[conf:high]` = URL and/or ATS verified this session; `[conf:med]` = careers root is canonical and stable, ATS/tenant inferred from strong pattern evidence; `[conf:low]` = careers root plausible, must be resolved before hardcoding. Never hardcode a `[conf:low]` tenant slug — resolve it by loading the company's careers root and following the first "Apply" link, which reveals the ATS host.

**Ingestion notes**

*Reduce ~200 employers to 8 adapters.* The entire section collapses into a small set of ATS clients:

- **Workday** (~35–40% of this family). URL shape `https://<tenant>.wd{1,2,3,5,101,103}.myworkdayjobs.com/<site>`. The machine endpoint is `POST https://<tenant>.wd<N>.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site>/jobs` with JSON body `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":0,"searchText":"intern"}`; response has `total` and `jobPostings[]` each with `externalPath`, `title`, `locationsText`, `postedOn`, `bulletFields`. Detail: `GET /wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site><externalPath>`. `limit` is capped at 20 — paginate on `offset`. Verified tenants in this section: `target/targetcareers` (wd5), `humana/Humana_External_Career_Site` (wd5), `iqvia/IQVIA` (wd1), `massgeneralbrigham/MGBExternal` (wd1), `mymvw/MVW` (wd5).
- **Phenom People** (~20%). Recognizable by `/main/jobs/<numeric-id>?lang=en-us` (PepsiCo) or `/<region>/en/job/<reqid>/<slug>` (P&G, Nike) and a `/us/en/search-results?keywords=…&category=…` search page (Humana, Marriott). Machine endpoint is `POST https://<careers-domain>/widgets` with `{"lang":"en_us","deviceType":"desktop","country":"us","pageName":"search-results","ddoKey":"refineSearch","from":0,"size":10,"keywords":"intern","jobs":true,"counts":true}`. Confirm the exact payload against a live site before hardcoding — Phenom versions drift. The `?keywords=intern` HTML search-results page is always a reliable fallback.
- **Radancy TalentBrew** (~10%). Job canonical `https://<domain>/en/job/<city-slug>/<title-slug>/<orgId>/<jobId>`. Search fragment (verified against Disney): `GET https://<domain>/en/search-jobs/results?ActiveFacetID=0&CurrentPage=1&RecordsPerPage=15&Distance=50&RadiusUnitType=0&Keyword=intern&ShowRadius=False&IsPagination=False&FacetType=0&SearchResultsModuleName=Search+Results&SearchFiltersModuleName=Search+Filters&SortCriteria=0&SortDirection=0&SearchType=5&OrganizationIds=<orgId>` — returns an **HTML fragment, not JSON**; parse with a DOM parser and paginate on `CurrentPage`. Known org ids: Disney `391`, Cargill `23251`.
- **Eightfold AI** (growing fast — Starbucks, John Deere). `GET https://<host>/api/apply/v2/jobs?domain=<corp-domain>&start=0&num=10&query=intern&sort_by=relevance`. **Verified 403 on a bare client** — you must send a realistic browser `User-Agent` and a same-origin `Referer`. Response has `count` and `positions[]`. `num` tops out around 10–20; paginate on `start`.
- **Greenhouse** (health/genomics/digital-health tail). `GET https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<token>/jobs?content=true` — unauthenticated, no rate limit worth worrying about, returns `{"jobs":[…],"meta":{"total":N}}`. **Verified tokens in this section:** `8451university` (84.51°), `flatironhealth`, `doximity`, `natera`, `10xgenomics`. **Verified-dead tokens (do not retry):** `tempus`, `tempusai`, `23andme`, `verily`, `includedhealth`, `guardanthealth` — all 404. Resolve these from the company careers page's Apply link.
- **SmartRecruiters** (Amadeus and some EU CPG). `GET https://api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/<companyId>/postings?limit=100&offset=0`, then `GET /postings/<id>` for detail. Public and unauthenticated.
- **Avature** (Epic, some pharma). `https://<tenant>.avature.net/careers` with `SearchJobs` paths. Session-cookie heavy; often better to scrape the marketing site (`careers.epic.com/jobs/intern/`) and follow through only at apply time.
- **iCIMS / Jibe** (McDonald's-style hashed job ids, many health systems). Job canonical `https://<domain>/<slug>/job/<32-char uppercase hex>` or `https://careers-<tenant>.icims.com/jobs/<id>/<slug>/job`. Search: `https://careers-<tenant>.icims.com/jobs/search?ss=1&searchKeyword=intern`. iCIMS is the most bot-hostile of the group.

*Universal fallbacks.* Every one of these portals ships a `sitemap.xml` (often `/sitemap_jobs.xml` or `/en/sitemap.xml`). For any employer whose ATS you cannot classify, crawl the sitemap and diff it daily — this catches new reqs with zero reverse-engineering. Second fallback: most of these companies syndicate to Google Jobs, so the structured `JobPosting` JSON-LD block is present in the page `<head>` even when the search API is opaque — extract that.

*Rate limits and anti-bot posture.* Greenhouse and SmartRecruiters: effectively unlimited, be polite (1–2 req/s). Workday CXS: no published limit but tenants sit behind Akamai; keep to ~1 req/s per tenant and vary nothing else — it is stable. Eightfold: **UA-gated (403)**, otherwise permissive. Phenom: Cloudflare/Akamai in front of some tenants; `/widgets` POSTs from a plain HTTP client sometimes get challenged — headless fallback needed for maybe 20% of Phenom sites. Radancy: permissive. iCIMS and Avature: expect bot challenges and session cookies; budget headless. Walmart's careers SPA and CVS's portal are the two most reliably headless-only sites in this section.

*Seasonal calendar (US, for a summer internship).* **Aug**: Walmart opens (early Aug); PepsiCo opened Aug 25 in the 2026 cycle. **Sept**: the peak month — Target, Nike, Disney Technology (announcement blog posts early Sept, reqs follow), John Deere, Cargill, UnitedHealth (mid-Sept), Marriott, most CPG. **Oct**: PepsiCo closes (Oct 31), UHG priority deadline, most retail/CPG close; ag closes after the fall career-fair circuit. **Nov**: UHG and most payers close by mid-Nov. **Nov–Feb**: pharma/biotech second wave — Merck, BMS, Amgen, Genentech, Vertex, Moderna post heavily Dec–Feb; PhD/R&D roles run earlier than IT roles. **Jan–Mar**: health systems and hospitals post summer roles *very* late — Mayo, Cleveland Clinic, MGB, and the BCBS plans are still opening reqs in February when tech companies have finished. **Rolling / off-cycle**: Epic Systems (year-round, fast decisions), Veeva, Wayfair, CarMax, and most digital-health startups. **Disney College Program**: separate rolling cycle opening roughly Feb/Mar and Aug/Sept — again, not technical.

**Traps & dead ends**

- **Disney College Program is not a technical internship.** It is park/resort operations. It shares the Disney careers domain and outranks the real Disney Technology Internships in search. Filter it out explicitly (`/disney-programs` path) or your pipeline will bury a T1 source under hundreds of hospitality reqs.
- **"Walmart Labs" no longer exists** as a brand — rebranded to Walmart Global Tech in 2019. Searching for "Walmart Labs internship" returns only stale third-party content.
- **84.51° does not post on Kroger's careers site.** It has its own Greenhouse board (`8451university`). Any pipeline that enumerates "Kroger" and stops there misses the single best data-science internship in grocery. The board is also *empty most of the year* — a zero-result response is not a broken adapter, it is off-season. Poll from August.
- **Invitae is dead** (Ch. 11 Feb 2024, assets to Labcorp Aug 2024). **Change Healthcare is dead** as an employer (absorbed into Optum Insight; `careers.changehealthcare.com` gone). **Cerner is dead** as a brand (Oracle Health; `cerner.com/careers` redirects to Oracle). **23andMe filed Ch. 11 in March 2025** and its hiring is a shadow of its former self. **Kellogg Company split** into Kellanova and WK Kellogg Co (2023) — one careers site became two. **Anthem is now Elevance Health** (2022). **Overstock is now Beyond Inc.** (2023). **PerkinElmer is now Revvity** (2023). Every one of these will produce dead adapters if taken from a pre-2023 company list.
- **Roche and Genentech share requisitions.** So do Yum! Brands and Taco Bell, and Marriott International and Marriott Vacations Worldwide (which are, confusingly, *different companies* with different Workday tenants). Build dedupe on requisition id, not on title+company.
- **Aggregator mirror farms are worse than useless here.** Prosple, Internship Sphere, InternshipsOrbit, Zippia, ZipRecruiter reposts, TheMuse mirrors, and "Extern"/"Cloudsoftsol"-type SEO blogs all rank highly for these queries and carry stale, partially fabricated, or LLM-generated listing details (including invented deadlines and salary bands). Use them only for *discovery of a program's existence*, never as a posting source, and never trust their dates.
- **Glassdoor/Indeed comp data on this family is systematically wrong.** Chick-fil-A's SWE intern posting was scraped as "$92K to $155K"; Walmart's intern rate shows as both $52/hr and "$19–35/hr" depending on which corporate tree the scraper hit. Do not ingest third-party comp for non-tech employers.
- **Hospital "IT internship" postings are frequently unpaid, for-credit, or restricted** to students enrolled in a health-informatics or HIM degree program. Parse eligibility text before surfacing; the pay-vs-credit signal is the single most important field in this sub-cluster.
- **CPG "IT Internship" is often not software engineering.** A large share are SAP/ERP functional-analyst, business-systems, or IT-project-management roles. They are still valuable and still low-competition, but tag them distinctly or the student will misjudge fit.
- **Property-level vs. corporate reqs swamp hospitality feeds.** Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, and the cruise lines post thousands of hotel/ship operational roles for every corporate tech req. Filter on HQ city (Bethesda, McLean, Chicago, Miami) or the IT/Technology category facet *before* storing, or you will 100× your database for nothing.
- **`careers.<company>.com` is not always the requisition system.** Many of these are marketing shells (Walmart, Target, Deere, Cargill, Humana all have a marketing layer over a different ATS). Always follow one Apply link to discover the real host before writing an adapter — and re-check annually, because ATS migrations in this family are frequent (Deere→Eightfold, Starbucks→Eightfold, MGB→Workday are all recent).
- **Costco, Ross, Burlington, and Dollar Tree do not have meaningful tech-intern pipelines** despite enormous revenue. Documented so they are not re-investigated each cycle; check once a year, not weekly.
- **Do not assume the Greenhouse token equals the company slug.** Six of the tokens tested in this section 404'd on the obvious guess. Always resolve the token from a live Apply link.
