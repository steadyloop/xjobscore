## 1. General Job Aggregators, Meta-Search & Their APIs

This family is the widest net available: a handful of these sources index most of the postings that any US CS + Applied Math undergrad could plausibly apply to, including the technical roles buried inside banks, insurers, hospitals, utilities, defense primes and manufacturers that never show up on startup-focused boards. The catch is that the yield/effort curve is brutally bimodal — a small set of free structured APIs (Adzuna, USAJOBS, The Muse, Careerjet, Jooble, Arbeitnow, Himalayas, the Simplify GitHub feed) give you clean JSON for ~an hour of work each, while the highest-volume properties (Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Wellfound, LinkedIn beyond the guest endpoint) are behind Cloudflare/DataDome and cost either money or a permanent anti-bot maintenance tax. The 2024–2026 consolidation wave also gutted the middle of this market: Indeed's publisher API, ZipRecruiter's ZipSearch, Monster, CareerBuilder, Hired/Vettery, Otta and Proxycurl are all gone or absorbed, and re-investigating them is wasted effort. Treat this section as the **backfill/discovery layer** — high volume, heavy duplication, moderate freshness — and expect the actual apply-links to point back at the ATS sources covered elsewhere in the catalog.

All HTTP status codes below were probed live from a plain `curl` with a desktop Chrome UA on 2026-08-01. "403" means Cloudflare/DataDome challenge on a bare request, not that the site is down.

### 1.8 Google for Jobs & the SERP layer

*Architecture recommendation.* Normalize everything to `schema.org/JobPosting` on ingest. Dedupe on a composite key of `(normalized_company, normalized_title, normalized_location, coarse_date)` plus a canonicalized apply-URL — nearly every source in §1.1–§1.2 is re-syndicating the same ATS posting, so expect 60–80% cross-source duplication and treat the aggregator layer purely as a *discovery* channel that hands you ATS tokens for the direct-crawl fleet. Prefer whichever copy carries a `boards.greenhouse.io` / `jobs.lever.co` / `myworkdayjobs.com` apply URL as the canonical record. Honor `validThrough` for expiry.

*Seasonal calendar (US, for a CS + Applied Math undergrad).* **Summer internship postings for year N open in Jul–Sep of year N−1** — quant firms (Jane Street, Citadel, Optiver, SIG, Jump, DRW), then big tech, are the earliest movers, often July–August with rolling-close-when-full deadlines. **Aug–Oct is peak volume and peak urgency**; many top programs are effectively closed by early November. **Nov–Feb** is the second wave: mid-size tech, finance/insurance/healthcare technical internships, and most non-tech-company technical roles, which post far later than tech. **Jan–Mar** is federal season — USAJOBS Pathways Internship announcements cluster here and have hard `ApplicationCloseDate` values, often with 5–10 day open windows, so poll USAJOBS *daily* rather than weekly. **Mar–May** is the late/backfill wave plus most Fall/Spring co-op postings. **Fall co-ops** post Feb–Apr; **Spring co-ops** post Sep–Nov. Part-time/on-campus-adjacent roles are aseasonal but spike in August and January. Practical consequence: crank aggregate polling frequency 3–5× from mid-July through October, keep USAJOBS on a daily cadence year-round, and never let the Simplify `listings.json` feed go more than 24h stale during Aug–Nov.

**Traps & dead ends**

- **"Free Indeed API" content is almost entirely SEO chum.** A large fraction of 2026 blog posts advertising an "Indeed API" are lead-gen for third-party scrapers. Indeed's own APIs are all employer-side write APIs. If a page promises read access to Indeed listings with a free key, it is either reselling scraped data or wrong.
- **Indeed's Cloudflare wall is total, not rate-based.** As of late July 2026 even the bare homepage 403s from datacenter, residential, *and* stealth-proxy tiers. Do not spend days tuning headers — go through JobSpy's mobile-app path, Google for Jobs, or SimplyHired (same parent, no wall).
- **hiring.cafe's documented API is stale in every public tutorial.** Every GitHub scraper and Apify actor description still shows `POST /api/search-jobs` with a `searchState` body. That now returns **405**, and `GET` returns **401 Unauthorized**. Anything you copy from a tutorial will fail silently-ish. Re-capture from a live browser session before writing the adapter.
- **Glassdoor is not a distinct posting source anymore.** It merged into Indeed, Inc. and its listings are Indeed's. Paying an Apify actor to scrape Glassdoor jobs buys you duplicates. Its reviews and salary data are still differentiated; its *postings* are not.
- **Adzuna's free API has no internship filter, and neither do most aggregator APIs.** `contract_time=part_time` is not "internship". Only The Muse (`level=Internship`), Careerjet (`contracttype=i`), JSearch (`employment_types=INTERN`), LinkedIn (`f_E=1`/`f_JT=I`), Dice (`filters.employmentType=INTERNSHIP`), and SimplyHired (`job_types=internship`) have real ones. Everywhere else you are doing keyword matching — and "intern" naively matched will pull in "internal", "international", and "internist". Anchor on word boundaries and add `co-op`, `co op`, `coop`, `summer analyst`, `industrial placement`, `apprentice`, `student worker`, `new grad`, `university graduate`.
- **Trovit / Mitula / Jobrapido / JobisJob / Recruit.net / Juju are ~pure re-syndication.** Building six adapters here yields almost no unique postings after dedupe. Build one (WhatJobs or Jobrapido) as a duplication canary and skip the rest.
- **`JobisJob` returns 200 but an almost-empty body** (~3KB) — the results arrive by XHR. A naive HTTP adapter will look like it works and silently produce zero rows. Same failure mode lurks on several sites in §1.2; always assert a minimum result count in your ingester.
- **Careerjet's API fails without a `Referer` header** with a confusing "Undeclared referrer" error rather than a 401. Multiple public clients omit it. Similarly, USAJOBS requires your *email address* in the `User-Agent` header — send a browser UA and you get a 401 that looks like a bad key.
- **`arbeitnow.com` without `www` 301-redirects**, which silently drops POST bodies and custom headers in some HTTP clients. Always use the `www` host.
- **RemoteOK's first array element is not a job** — it's a legal/attribution notice. Ingesting it produces a garbage row on every poll.
- **Auto-apply tools (LazyApply, Sonara, Massive) are not data sources.** They have no readable index, they cost money, and mass auto-submission actively damages a candidate's reputation with the exact employers this student wants. Explicitly out of scope.
- **The "publisher program" tables on aggregator marketing sites are stale.** Indeed's and ZipRecruiter's pages still describe programs that are closed. Talent.com and WhatJobs are the two in this section I could confirm are actively recruiting publishers in 2026.
- **Common Crawl is not a freshness source.** Its snapshots lag by weeks-to-months and internship postings expire in days. Use it for career-page/ATS-tenant *discovery* only, then crawl those hosts live.
- **openroles' data is CC BY-SA 4.0.** If xjobscore ever publishes a derived dataset, share-alike attaches. Keep it in a separately-licensed table if that matters.
- **`Serper.dev` reportedly has no Jobs vertical** — Google web results only. Cheap, but possibly useless for this specific job. Verify before adopting it as the SERP backend.
- **Don't reuse the example `affid` values that appear in Careerjet's client READMEs.** They work (that's how I confirmed the API is live) but they belong to someone else and the traffic is attributed to them. Open a partner account.
