## 9. Government, National Labs, Public Sector & Cleared Defense Research

This is the single largest under-applied-to pool available to a US-citizen CS + Applied Math undergrad, and it is almost entirely invisible to Handshake/LinkedIn-style scraping because it lives on ~200 disconnected portals with idiosyncratic ATSs and application deadlines 6–14 months ahead of the start date. Yield is very high per application (federal internships routinely see 10–50x fewer applicants than a FAANG SWE req), but the effort profile is front-loaded: the calendar, not the search, is the hard part — miss October–January and 70% of this section is gone for the year. Two structural facts make it machine-tractable: (a) USAJOBS has a real public API that covers every competitive-service posting across all agencies, and (b) Zintellect/ORISE, the DOE WDTS umbrella, and a handful of Workday/SuccessFactors/iCIMS tenants collapse dozens of programs into a few scrapable endpoints. Citizenship is the dominant filter — assume US citizenship required unless a row says otherwise, and assume clearance-eligibility (no dual citizenship complications, no recent foreign contacts) for anything in 9.6–9.8 and 9.12.

**Ingestion notes**

*Hardcode these endpoint templates.* (1) **USAJOBS**: `GET https://data.usajobs.gov/api/search?HiringPath=student&HiringPath=graduates&ResultsPerPage=500&Page=N` with headers `Host: data.usajobs.gov`, `User-Agent: <registered email>`, `Authorization-Key: <key>`; verified 401 without the key, verified 200 on the unauthenticated `/api/codelist/*` family. Also filter by `JobCategoryCode` on the occupational series 2210/1550/1520/1529/1530/0854/1515. Pull `MatchedObjectDescriptor.UserArea.Details` for the "Who May Apply" and clearance text — the top-level fields do not carry it. (2) **Workday CxS** (the single most reusable pattern here): `POST https://<tenant>.wd<N>.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site>/jobs` with body `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":<N>,"searchText":"intern"}`; detail via `GET /wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site><externalPath>`. Verified live: **Federal Reserve System** `rb`/`FRS` (75 intern hits, covers all 12 banks + Board), **NREL** `nrel`/`NLR` (note the `NLR` site name from the 2026 rename), **Fermilab** `fermilab`/`FermilabCareers`, **Brookhaven** `bnl`/`Externa` (the site really is spelled "Externa"). An unknown tenant/site returns 422, which is a cheap way to probe. (3) **Taleo** (LBNL `jobs.lbl.gov`, Texas `capps.taleo.net`): `careersection/<section>/jobsearch.ftl` with form-post paging. (4) **SuccessFactors** (ORNL): `https://jobs.ornl.gov/search/?q=intern&startrow=N` — verified 200. (5) **iCIMS** (PNNL `careers.pnnl.gov`): probe the `?in_iframe=1` render and the `/jobs/search` variants; iCIMS often exposes an RSS. (6) **Zintellect**: `https://www.zintellect.com/Opportunity/Details/<ORG>-<PROGRAM>-<YEAR>[-TERM]` — verified for `ORNL-PCIP-2026`, `ORNL-RSI-2026`, `ORNL-TPI-2026`, `DHS-POWER-2026-SUMMER`; you can pre-generate next cycle's URLs by incrementing the year. (7) **NEOGOV**: `https://www.governmentjobs.com/careers/<slug>` — verified for washington, massachusetts, maryland, georgia, northcarolina, michigan, newjersey, ohio; enumerate remaining slugs from `/careers/home`.

*Anti-bot posture.* A large share of `.gov`/`.mil` sits behind Akamai or CloudFront and returns **403 to a default curl UA**: nsa.gov, fbijobs.gov, nro.gov, dia.mil, iarpa.gov, inl.gov, anl.gov, pppl.gov, netl.doe.gov, wrp.gov, bls.gov, gao.gov, sec.gov, dot.gov, peacecorps.gov, mass.gov, mitre.org, talent.aerospace.org, afrlscholars.usra.edu, codeforamerica.org. A realistic browser User-Agent fixed most of these in testing; the remainder need a headless browser. jlab.org returned **429** on a single request — rate-limit aggressively (≥2s between requests, per-host). nasa.gov's `intern.nasa.gov` Gateway is a JS SPA and requires HEADLESS; scrape the deadline table from the static `nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/` page instead. NSF domains (`nsf.gov`, `etap.nsf.gov`) failed to resolve entirely from this environment — treat as unverified rather than dead.

*Seasonal calendar (the actual product of this section).* **Aug**: SMART opens Aug 1; WRP student registration opens ~Aug 24; NGA posts next-summer internships Aug–Sept; NSA college applications open. **Sept**: NSA Stokes Sept 15–Oct 31; DOE SULI spring-term deadline Sept 30; NASA spring deadline ~Sept 14; State Dept summer internships close Sept/Oct; PMF opens. **Oct**: DHS HS-POWER opens Oct 1; NOAA Hollings opens Oct 1; AFRL Scholars opens Oct 10; NSA DSP ~Oct 15; IDA begins rolling selections; NREIP opens. **Nov–Dec**: RAND Dec 1; SMART closes first Friday of Dec; CIA DO undergrad opens Dec 15; CISA SFS opens Dec 15; NIH SIP opens ~Dec 8; Coding it Forward opens. **Jan** (the densest month by far): DOE SULI summer ~Jan 7; DHS HS-POWER ~Jan 5; AFRL Scholars closes Jan 10; Coding it Forward ~Jan 19; NIST SURF Jan 26; CIA closes Jan 30; ORNL PCIP Jan 30; NOAA Hollings Jan 31; LLNL DSSI, LANL Parallel Computing, CBO, Library of Congress Junior Fellows all ~January. **Feb**: NIH SIP Feb 18; CISA SFS Feb 27; NASA summer Feb 27; Aspen Feb 5. **Mar–May**: MIT Lincoln Lab SRP postings run Jan–Mar for that same summer (the main late-cycle rescue); APL early-consideration ~Mar 31; **FBI Honors opens mid-April and closes ~May 2 — for the summer 13 months later**; NASA fall ~May 21; SULI fall ~May 20. **Jun–Jul**: Argonne fall seasonal ~Jun 12; VSFS projects go live Jul 1 with applications through ~Jul 31. Net effect: **the practical annual deadline for this entire section is roughly Sept 1 – Feb 1**, and a pipeline that only starts polling in February has already lost the year.

*Citizenship/clearance encoding.* Add three boolean-ish fields per row and populate them at ingest: `citizenship_required` (US-citizen-only: all of 9.6, 9.7, 9.8, MIT LL, MITRE, APL, Aerospace, IDA, CNA, SEI cleared work, VSFS, NASA OSTEM, NOAA Hollings), `lpr_ok` (green card accepted: DOE SULI/CCI, NIST SURF, NIH SIP, most DOE lab unclassified roles, some NREIP labs), `clearance_required` (Secret / TS / TS-SCI+poly: everything in 9.6, most of 9.7, MIT LL, much of 9.12). Also capture `polygraph` separately — NSA/CIA/NGA/NRO/DIA all require one and it adds 6–12 months of processing that determines when you must apply.

**Traps & dead ends**

The biggest trap is **applying on the calendar you're used to**. Corporate SWE internships open Aug–Oct for the next summer; a large fraction of this section opens 12–14 months out (FBI Honors: apply April 2026 for summer 2027) or closes in a window you'd never think to check (NSA Stokes: Sept 15–Oct 31 only). A pipeline optimized for "new postings this week" will systematically miss this domain — you need a **deadline calendar**, and for several programs a **reminder to apply before the posting even exists**.

The second trap is **assuming USAJOBS is the government**. It is only the *competitive civil service*. It does not contain: the Federal Reserve System (own Workday), any FFRDC or UARC (MITRE, Lincoln Lab, APL, Aerospace, IDA, RAND, CNA, SEI, Draper), any DOE national lab's own reqs (contractor-operated — only NETL is federal), NASA JPL (Caltech-operated), the CIA (own system), or any state/local government. Roughly half the rows above are invisible to a USAJOBS-only crawler.

Third: **program name churn**. HS-STEM→HS-POWER, SPAWAR→NIWC, USDS→U.S. DOGE Service, Aspen Tech Policy Hub→Aspen Policy Academy, NREL's Workday site→`NLR`, NSIN's `.mil`→`.us`. University career-center pages are the worst offenders for propagating dead names — they are cached for years and rank well. Always resolve to the primary source.

Fourth: **the SEO aggregator layer**. Searching any program in this section returns internshipsphere.net, internshiparena.com, opportunitydesk.org, o3schools, grantsdatabase.org and a dozen similar sites before the primary source. They restate deadlines from two cycles ago and frequently link to 404s. They are worth exactly one thing — surfacing a program name you didn't know existed — and nothing else.

Fifth: **eligibility cliffs that are invisible until you apply**. NSA Stokes is for *incoming* college freshmen. SEAP (Navy) is high-school-only. PMF and RAND's Summer Associate program are graduate-only. NOAA Hollings requires you to apply as a *sophomore*. CIA's DO Undergraduate Internship requires you to have completed only *one* semester at the time of expressing interest. JHU APL's ASPIRE is high-school; RISE@APL is JHU-students-only. Encode `class_year_eligibility` as a first-class field or you will burn a cycle.

Sixth: **unpaid and non-job "opportunities" masquerading as internships**. VSFS is unpaid. USDR is volunteer. Smithsonian has both paid and unpaid units. JHU APL's ASPIRE is explicitly unpaid and requires 30–40 hrs/week on-site. CyberForce, the NSA Codebreaker Challenge, and Hacking for Defense are recruiting funnels, not employment. Keep a `compensation` field and don't let unpaid rows dilute the ranked list.

Finally: **budget and political volatility, 2025–2026**. NOAA's EPP/MSI scholarship had no 2026 class due to budget uncertainty. USAID was dismantled. USDS became DOGE. PIF and U.S. Digital Corps cohorts were disrupted by 2025 terminations. CFPB's continued operation has been contested. Several DOE program pages moved or 403'd during verification. Anything in this section marked T1/T2 should carry a `last_verified` timestamp and be re-checked at the start of each application cycle rather than trusted from a prior year's crawl.
