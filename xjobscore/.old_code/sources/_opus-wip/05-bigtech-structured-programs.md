## 5. Big Tech & Named Structured / Early-Identity Programs

This is the family with the worst aggregator coverage and the best payoff per unit of effort. Named early-identity programs (Google STEP, Microsoft Explore, Meta University, Amazon Propel, Uber STAR) are the single highest-leverage thing a rising sophomore/junior can apply to — they are explicitly scoped to 1st/2nd-year students, they compete against a far smaller pool than the general SWE intern req, and **they almost never appear on Indeed/LinkedIn/Simplify**; they live on a program microsite plus one requisition on the employer's own ATS that opens for four to eight weeks and closes. The other half of this section is the brute-force half: the direct careers portal of every major tech employer, mapped to the ATS behind it so Section 2's endpoint machinery applies without new code. Everything below with a job-count was live-verified by direct HTTP on **2026-08-01**; counts are total open reqs (all roles, not just intern) and are the freshness/liveness signal, not a coverage claim.

The seasonal spine to internalize: **the Summer 2027 cycle opens July–September 2026 for most of this list**, closes on a rolling basis (many close within 2–4 weeks of opening, Google STEP historically closes in October), and a smaller second wave reopens January–March. If the pipeline only polls quarterly it will miss the majority of these.

**Ingestion notes**

*Endpoint templates worth hardcoding.* Five patterns cover ~85% of the rows above and all map onto your existing `seeds/ats.tsv`: Greenhouse `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{token}/jobs?content=true`; Ashby `https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{token}?includeCompensation=true`; Lever `https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/{token}?mode=json` (the `categories.commitment == "Intern"` field is the cleanest programmatic class-year filter in the whole catalog); Workday `POST https://{tenant}.{wdhost}.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/{tenant}/{site}/jobs` with body `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":N,"searchText":""}` and `Content-Type: application/json` — **it must be POST, GET returns 405**, `limit` is capped at 20 server-side, and the response `total` field lets you pre-compute page count; Eightfold `https://{careers_host}/api/apply/v2/jobs?domain={domain}&start=N&num=100`, keyed on the company's **domain** rather than a board token, which is why it cannot be derived from a posting URL and needs a separate discovery path.

*Four employer-specific endpoints worth hardcoding as one-offs.* Amazon: `https://www.amazon.jobs/en/search.json?base_query={q}&result_limit=100&offset={n}&job_type=Intern` — the richest single first-party feed in this section, and `basic_qualifications`/`preferred_qualifications` come inline so class-year eligibility ("currently enrolled in a Bachelor's… graduating May 2028") is parseable without fetching the detail page. Uber: `POST https://www.uber.com/api/loadSearchJobsResults?localeCode=en` with `{"params":{"page":N},"limit":100}` and any non-empty `x-csrf-token` header. Google: HTML at `https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results?target_level=INTERN_AND_APPRENTICE&page={n}` — server-rendered, so no headless, and the numeric job id in the URL slug is a stable key. Texas Instruments: `https://edbz.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmRestApi/resources/latest/recruitingCEJobRequisitions?onlyData=true&finder=findReqs;siteNumber=CX` — this is the Oracle ORC pattern your `seeds/ats.tsv` already models, with the FA host and site number now resolved.

*Rate limits and anti-bot posture.* Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever were all uncomplaining across ~150 sequential requests with no key and no delay — treat them as effectively unmetered but stay under ~5 req/s out of courtesy. Workday CXS is tolerant but returns **422 on a wrong site name and 401 on a valid tenant with a wrong site** — that distinction is a useful discovery signal (401 means "keep guessing the site, the tenant is real"). Meta returned **429 within about fifteen requests** and is the tightest limiter here; budget one request every few seconds with exponential backoff. Cloudflare or equivalent bot walls returned 403/406/202 on `pinterestcareers.com`, `coinbase.com/careers`, `uber.com` HTML, `intel.com/jobs`, `marvell.com`, `careers.etsy.com`, `about.coursera.org`, and `hashicorp.com` — in every one of those cases there is a clean ATS endpoint behind the wall, so **never scrape the marketing careers page when an ATS token exists**. TikTok/ByteDance failed to connect at all from a US datacenter IP (code 000, not a 403), which implies network-level filtering rather than bot detection; that one needs residential egress or should be dropped.

*Seasonal calendar for this family.* **June–August:** NVIDIA, Cloudflare opens; Palantir opens (historically August); Google STEP NA/EU window opens; general Big Tech Summer-2027 reqs begin appearing. **August–October:** the peak — Amazon SDE Intern, Meta SWE Intern, Microsoft Explore (rolling from ~July), Adobe, MongoDB, Duolingo, Riot, Spotify, Atlassian, Stripe, Databricks all open, and most close on a rolling basis within weeks. **September:** Meta University opens on its own later track; MSR and Adobe Research open their research cycles. **September–November:** Amazon Propel; Google STEP closes (historically October). **October–November:** Palantir Winter Fellowship + Winter Defense Tech Fellowship applications for a December–January sprint. **January–March:** the second wave — Google STEP rolling remainder, Amazon Future Engineer scholarship (Jan–Feb), leftover reqs across the board, plus most **Fall/Winter co-op** reqs. **February–April:** IBM Accelerate (off-cycle spring open for a June start) and Google Summer of Code contributor applications (late March to early April, immovable). **May–July:** the quiet trough for summer reqs, but the right time to catch off-cycle programs — Pinterest's apprenticeship closed in early July for an October start, which a summer-only poller misses entirely. Practical consequence: poll the T1 rows **daily from July through November**, weekly the rest of the year, and never let the pipeline assume "internship = summer start."

**Traps & dead ends**

The single biggest trap is **token-guessing against ATSes that do not 404**. SmartRecruiters returns `200 {"totalFound":0}` for every tenant name I invented, so a status-code-only validity check marks all of them live. Ashby is subtler: `bogusxyz123` correctly 404s, but a *real but abandoned* board like `vercel` returns `200 {"jobs":[]}` forever. Same for Lever `plaid`. Your validity predicate for this family must be "HTTP 200 **and** at least one posting, re-checked over time", never status alone.

The second trap is **assuming the obvious token**. Verified counterexamples: DoorDash is `doordashusa` not `doordash`; Box is `boxinc`; Glean is `gleanwork`; Couchbase is `couchbaseinc`; Sigma is `sigmacomputing`; C3.ai is `c3iot` (legacy name); Chroma is `trychroma`; Character.AI is `character`; Cursor is `cursor` not `anysphere`; Mistral's token contains a literal dot (`mistral.ai`); Applied Materials' Workday tenant is `amat`; Marvell's Workday *site* is `MarvellCareers` not `External`; Cadence's is `External_Careers`; Expedia and Alteryx both live on the unusual `wd108` host. Every one of these fails silently as a 404 that looks like "company not on this ATS."

The third trap is **the marketing careers page**. Nearly every "students" landing page I tested is either 404 (Micron, Applied Materials, Qualcomm, AMD, Shopify, Roblox), redirected to a generic hub (IBM Accelerate and Extreme Blue both collapse to `ibm.com/careers`), or bot-walled (Pinterest, Intel, Coinbase, Uber). These pages are for humans and change constantly; the ATS token behind them is stable for years. Use the landing pages **only** as program-discovery signals and calendar oracles, never as job sources.

Fourth: **Eightfold does not generalize.** Netflix's public `api/apply/v2/jobs` works beautifully; Qualcomm's and Lam Research's return `403 {"message":"Not authorized for PCSX"}` on both the vanity host and the `*.eightfold.ai` host. Do not write an Eightfold adapter that assumes a working public API — probe per tenant and fall back to headless.

Fifth: **defunct programs with living SEO.** Google CSSI was discontinued around 2022 but still has 2025-dated "how to apply" blog posts ranking well; Adobe Digital Academy's domain no longer resolves; Shopify Dev Degree is alive but structurally narrowed (the Dominican University partnership ended in 2025). Any program row sourced from a listicle rather than the employer's own live site should be treated as unverified until the employer page confirms it.

Finally, **subsidiaries are not separate boards.** Looker reqs are Google reqs; Tableau, Slack, and MuleSoft reqs are Salesforce Workday reqs; Activision Blizzard is now partly inside Microsoft's stack; HashiCorp is migrating into IBM's. Building a separate crawler per brand produces duplicates, not coverage — dedupe on the ATS requisition id, and maintain a parent-company alias table (your `seeds/reseed_company_alias.sql` is the right place for it).
