## 2. ATS Platforms — The Direct-From-Employer Firehose

Every posting a student can apply to is born inside an applicant tracking system; aggregators are downstream copies with worse latency, worse fidelity, and hostile bot policy. Scraping the ATS gives you the requisition ID, the canonical apply URL, the full description, and — critically — a reliable *close* signal when a posting disappears from the board. The yield curve is brutally uneven: five vendors (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters) will cover ~70% of the CS-intern-relevant universe with clean unauthenticated JSON, another fifteen cover most of the rest with more work, and a long tail of ~60 vendors matters mainly because that is where **technical roles at non-tech companies** live — the bank, the hospital system, the utility, the ag-science firm, the state DOT. Those employers are almost never on Greenhouse; they are on Workday, iCIMS, Oracle ORC, SuccessFactors, Paycom, UKG, or NEOGOV. All endpoint statuses below were live-probed on **2026-08-01** unless marked otherwise.

**Ingestion notes**

Hardcode these templates; they are the ones verified live on 2026-08-01 and they are stable across tenants:
`https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{t}/jobs?content=false` (diff) and `?content=true` (fetch) · `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{t}` (liveness + display name) · `https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/{t}?mode=json` · `https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{t}?includeCompensation=true` · `POST https://{tenant}.{wdN}.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/{tenant}/{site}/jobs` with `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":N,"searchText":""}` · `https://api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/{c}/postings?limit=100&offset=N` · `https://apply.workable.com/api/v1/widget/accounts/{a}?details=true` · `https://{c}.bamboohr.com/careers/list` · `https://{c}.breezy.hr/json?verbose=true` · `https://ats.rippling.com/api/v2/board/{s}/jobs?page=N&pageSize=100` · `https://{c}.recruitee.com/api/offers/` · `https://{c}.pinpointhq.com/postings.json` · `https://{c}.jobs.personio.de/xml` · `https://{c}.teamtailor.com/jobs.json` · `GET https://{host}.oraclecloud.com/hcmRestApi/resources/latest/recruitingCEJobRequisitions?onlyData=true&finder=findReqs;siteNumber={CX_n},limit=25,offset=N` with header `ora-irc-language: en`.

Pagination models differ in kind, not degree, and getting this wrong silently truncates boards: **Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Breezy, Recruitee, Pinpoint, Personio return the entire board in one response** — no pagination at all, but payloads reach 12 MB (Ashby/`openai`) and 5.8 MB (Lever/`palantir`), and neither Lever nor Ashby gzips unless you send `Accept-Encoding: gzip`. **Workday caps `limit` at 20** and requires offset paging against the `total` field — a naive single request gets you 20 of 3,000 jobs. **SmartRecruiters caps `limit` at 100** and reports `totalFound`, so completeness is checkable. **Workable v1 reports no total at all**, which means it can never prove a complete listing; pair it with the v3 POST endpoint purely to get a count, and mark the run `complete=False` if they disagree. Rate limits: Ashby ~100 req/min informal (sleep 500–600 ms); Greenhouse and Lever tolerate steady polling but rate-limit per source IP under burst; iCIMS and Taleo rate-limit **per tenant domain**, so spread work across hosts rather than globally — which is convenient, since Workday, iCIMS, Oracle ORC, and Paylocity all put the tenant in the hostname and therefore self-distribute, while Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/SmartRecruiters concentrate all load on one API host and need per-host throttling (~1.5 s) instead.

Anti-bot posture is bimodal. The Tier-1 JSON APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday CXS, SmartRecruiters, Workable, BambooHR, Breezy, Rippling, Recruitee, Pinpoint, Personio, Teamtailor, Oracle ORC) served clean 200s to plain `curl` with only a browser `User-Agent` — no Cloudflare, no JS challenge, no cookie priming. Confirmed **403 Cloudflare challenges** on `www.catsone.com`, `hire.trakstar.com`, `app.polymer.co`, and `apply.indeed.com`. Taleo and PeopleSoft Candidate Gateway need session cookies and CSRF tokens from a prior GET. Cornerstone, SuccessFactors, Phenom, Eightfold-PCSX and Polymer render client-side and need either a headless browser or a per-tenant reverse-engineering pass to find the XHR. Always send `Accept: application/json` to BambooHR, Workday, and Rippling — they content-negotiate to HTML otherwise. Two `robots.txt` facts matter operationally: `jobs.lever.co` disallows CCBot (so Common Crawl discovery yields ~nothing for Lever), and NEOGOV's terms explicitly forbid scraping `governmentjobs.com/careers` for competing products.

Seasonality drives poll rates more than anything else. For a Summer 2027 internship the calendar is: **quant/HFT opens earliest** — Jane Street, Citadel, Optiver, IMC, Jump, DRW, SIG typically open May–September 2026 and fill on rolling review, sometimes closing within days; **big tech + AI labs** open September–November 2026 (Ashby and Greenhouse tenants dominate here); **defense primes, banks, and F500 non-tech** open October 2026–January 2027 on Workday/iCIMS/Avature/SuccessFactors, with a **second wave in February–March 2027** as headcount reshuffles; **national labs** (Workday tenants under M&O contractors, plus USAJOBS Pathways) have hard deadlines around October 2026 for Spring '27 and January 2027 for Summer '27, and are citizenship-gated; **university/on-campus and co-op roles** (Workday student sites, PeopleAdmin, Handshake) open late August 2026 and again in early January; **startups on Lever/Ashby/Workable/Rippling post continuously** with 2–6 week windows and no season at all — which is precisely why direct ATS polling beats any seasonal aggregator. Practical consequence: poll the startup ATSes every 15 minutes year-round, ramp the enterprise ATSes from daily to hourly across September–December, and treat national labs and IC portals as calendar watchers rather than pollers.

**Traps & dead ends**

The most expensive trap is the **recruitment-marketing skin**. Radancy/TalentBrew, Phenom, Symphony Talent, Clinch, and Beamery all render a branded careers site at `jobs.{company}.com` that *looks* like the system of record but is a front end; the Apply button hands off to Workday, BrassRing, or SuccessFactors underneath. Boeing, Lockheed, L3Harris, and Parsons all do this. If you bind the company to the skin you get a partial, stale board and an apply URL that breaks. **Always resolve the Apply destination before recording the ATS.** The mirror-image trap is assuming the skin is *only* a skin — Citigroup genuinely uses TalentBrew as its primary board.

A second trap is **assuming one tenant means one board**. Large Workday customers run four to eight sites, and the intern-relevant ones are separate: `Futureforce_Internships`, `University_Talent_Intern`, `Citi_Early_Careers_Events_Site`, `BlackRock_Early_Careers_Program`, `Workday_Early_Career`. Polling only `External` will miss most campus hiring at exactly the companies you most want. Related: some Workday sites are deliberately access-controlled and 403 by policy (`northrop_grumman_restricted_site`) — no token fixes that, and retrying wastes budget.

Do not build **enumeration** for anything. Tokens are opaque and often unrelated to the company name (`joinroot` for Root, `prentkeromich`, `mtesting.ai`), several vendors use compound keys (Workday `tenant/wdN/site`, iCIMS full host, Oracle `host/CX_n`, Comeet `uid + token`, BrassRing `partnerid + siteid`), and Paylocity/ADP/Paycom use **opaque UUIDs and 32-char hex client keys that cannot be guessed at all**. Enumeration is also a scan pattern concentrated on ~14 hosts, which is the fastest way to get blocked. Derivation from careers pages and Common Crawl spreads the same work across hundreds of hosts.

Beware **false liveness**. HTML-only vendors return a styled 200 "no such board" page rather than a 404 for wrong tokens (Jobvite, Teamtailor, iCIMS in some configurations), so an HTTP-status-only probe overcounts. Conversely a 403/429/timeout says nothing about whether the board exists and must never be recorded as absent — that is silent, permanent data loss. And an **empty board is still a live board**: Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby all return `200` with `[]` for a real tenant that has zero open roles, and it must keep being polled.

Finally, several plausible-looking paths are structurally closed and should not be revisited: every vendor's *official* API (iCIMS Search API, SuccessFactors OData, Cornerstone, Eightfold, Phenom, Bullhorn beyond the public job endpoint) requires OAuth credentials the **employer** issues, so no amount of signup gets you in. Freshteam is sunsetting and should get no adapter. JobFunnel is archived. JobSpy is a LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor scraper, not an ATS tool, and targets exactly the sites this project excludes. And the largest employers — Google, Amazon, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, Jane Street, Citadel — run no third-party ATS at all, so no entry in this catalog will ever reach them; they need bespoke in-house adapters, which belongs to a different section.
