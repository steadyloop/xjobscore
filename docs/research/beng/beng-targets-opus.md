## Window 4 — Right Now (Aug 2026 – Jun 2027): Pre-Graduation Experience
Even 10 hrs/week transforms every window above. Six months of clinical or quality experience is the great GPA neutralizer.

- **Part-time/per diem clinical research assistant** — UCSD Health, Moores Cancer Center, Rady Children's, Scripps (search job boards for "per diem" / "part-time CRC/CRA")
- **Paid part-time RA in UCSD labs** — email PIs in BENG/School of Medicine directly; ~10 emails typically yields 1–2 yeses
- **Part-time QA / document control** — small Sorrento Valley diagnostics and device companies
- **Eurofins (San Diego)** — hires part-time lab technicians

---

## Career Arc Notes (satisfaction + stability optimized)
- **Clinical track:** CRC/CTA → Clinical Research Associate (~2 yrs) → Clinical Operations management
- **Quality/regulatory track:** QA Associate → Quality Engineer → Regulatory Affairs (RAPS certification)
- Both tracks: structured, people-heavy, layoff-resistant, ~$120–160k mid-career.

## Strategy Summary
1. **Now:** Email PIs, apply to part-time clinical/QA roles, make senior design/capstone demo-ready.
2. **October:** UCSD fall career fair — target Tier 1/2 rotational programs in person.
3. **January:** Register with 3+ staffing agencies; begin weekly career-page checks.
4. **March–June:** Expect the offer from Window 2 just-in-time postings; Tier 3 hospital/CRO roles are the floor, everything above is upside.
