How counting works

A simple rule keeps the comparison useful and fair.

One application is counted when a teammate marks a tracked job Applied and adds a submission date. Moving a job between later stages never counts it again.

Private teammates are included only in the team total. Deleted test records and saved opportunities are excluded.
