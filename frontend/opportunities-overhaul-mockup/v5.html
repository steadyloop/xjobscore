Three ways to lose a row

The original works because it gives you two clean scan columns — company down one edge, title down the other — and because 14px title against a 10px location line makes the title read as the hero. The eyebrow broke both: it fused company with location so line 1 stopped being a column, and it closed the size gap so the title stopped ranking. The waste was never the row count — it is that lines 1 and 3 are each about 85% empty horizontally. Both candidates below spend that space instead of deleting a line of information.

A · Location right keeps line 1 exactly as you like it — one bold company name on the left edge — and puts the location on the right of that same line, where there is nothing. The company column survives intact, the title keeps its 14px-against-10px contrast, and the row disappears. Locations are a field you check once a title interests you, not one you scan, so right-alignment costs nothing.

B · Attribute bar folds location in with the skill pills, on the grounds that neither is scanned and both are things you filter on. Line 1 and the title are untouched. It only saves a row on the 80% of cards that have skills — the other 20% still need the bar for location alone.

C · Eyebrow is the rejected version, kept so the comparison is honest. Watch line 1 stop being a column.

Every candidate shows the same eleven postings, real titles and locations from the feed, ending with the two stress cases: a five-city Cigna programme and the 76-character Aston Martin one where a British region resolved to a town in Arkansas. Locations cap at 2 with a +N overflow throughout.
