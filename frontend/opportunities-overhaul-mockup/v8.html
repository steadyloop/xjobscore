The gap was never a card problem. With a right-hand rail the location sits wherever column 1 ends, so at 700px the gap after a short company name is 402px — narrowing from 780 had moved it 80px. Switch between Stacked and Rail at 700 and then at 500 to see that the width is doing all the work.

Why 500px. A median title is 47 characters, about 324px. At 700px the card row is 620px wide, so 296px of it is empty on a typical card. At 500px the row is 420px and the slack is 96px — enough to breathe, not enough to strand anything. The cost is title wrapping, which the readout tracks.

The pane gets what the feed gives back. The list holds short strings you scan; the pane holds paragraphs you read. It had 470px against the feed's 700 — backwards. Here it takes up to 720px, which is why it now reaches down the page instead of stopping at 40%.

Stacked is the shipping card, unchanged: company, title, location, skills. In a column sized to its content there is no far edge to be thrown to, so the thing you objected to stops existing.
